How the comparison works

Starting volume is the average of the 5–6 recorded months immediately before each shop’s calling start date. The launch month is skipped. Follow-up is the average of the next 3 or 6 full calendar months, with the same shops included throughout the selected period.

Each shop’s growth = (follow-up monthly average ÷ starting monthly average − 1) × 100. Average growth gives every matched shop equal weight. The illustration applies that percentage to your entered count; it is an average monthly volume across the selected period, not an end-of-period forecast.

These are observed before-and-after results. Seasonality and other business changes can affect car counts; the comparison does not isolate the effect of calling or guarantee future results. Small samples and flagged counts need care. No missing month is replaced with zero.

Source data and exclusions
