HOW TO READ THIS COMPARISON

The rules behind the graph

Dates from the history

A shop’s first appearance in “past numbers” is its first active calendar month. Every visit month from launch through the selected horizon must be present and valid. Missing months exclude a shop for that period.

Comparable size

Column G provides car count. Blank, zero, negative, fractional counts, and months with BDC visits above total cars are not used to assign size. At least three valid months are required. Later car counts may classify earlier visit history.

Reported monthly visits

The graph uses column D, “Total BDC Visits.” These are historical reported totals, not a reconstruction of the main report’s deduplicated, 30-day RO attribution. They are not added to the main report’s revenue or visit totals.

Reporting exclusions

Hughes Motors Auto Repair is excluded from all car-count comparisons because its processes make car counts unreliable. Tomah MN/WI records remain separate and excluded pending confirmation of location identity and launch date. Duplicate shop-months also exclude a shop.

A recorded zero BDC visit count is valid. Start dates reflect calendar months, not exact launch days. Complete records do not independently prove full-month operation, equal outreach effort, or consistent historical visit definitions. Seasonality, service mix, and shop differences remain; these descriptive graphs do not establish incremental lift or predict future results. Counts failing validation stay in the source download for review. Other reported activity for Hughes may remain in the original report.
