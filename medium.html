04 / NUMBERS YOU CAN STAND BEHIND

Trust starts with the counting rules.

01

One RO. One revenue credit.

RO and appointment IDs are keyed by shop, so identical IDs at different shops stay separate. Repeated ROs count once across services and months. Exact duplicate contact rows are removed.

02

A strict 30-day window.

An RO qualifies when its opening date is on or after an explicitly linked revenue-service contact, within 30 calendar days. We credit the latest eligible revenue contact. Same-day activity qualifies because the RO has no time of day.

03

Dollars with evidence.

Revenue comes from posted ROs with a uniquely identifiable individual dollar amount. Combined multi-RO amounts are not divided or counted again. Missing opening dates, future ROs, and unpriced ROs receive no revenue credit.

04

Time to convert.

Raw conversion is observed converted tasks divided by unique revenue tasks. The mature cohort rate uses tasks whose last contact is at least 30 days before October 31, 2026. No uplift is applied to recent tasks.
